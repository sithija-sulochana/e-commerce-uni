Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, returns, and more

Account & Login

To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of our website. Fill in your email address, create a strong password, and provide your basic information. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account. Once verified, you can start shopping!

Click on the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. Click the link in the email and follow the instructions to create a new password.

Products & Orders

After your order is shipped, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your package on our "Order Tracking" page. Simply enter your tracking number to see real-time updates on your delivery status.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), digital wallets (PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay), and bank transfers. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 business days. Overnight delivery is also an option for most locations. Shipping times are calculated from the date your order is confirmed and processed.

We currently ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location. During checkout, you can select your country and see the exact shipping cost and estimated delivery date before completing your purchase.

Most of our products come with a manufacturer's warranty. Laptops typically have 1-2 years, and smartphones have 1 year. Check the product details page for specific warranty information. We also offer extended warranty options at checkout.

Returns & Refunds

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on most items. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund. Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories included.

Log in to your account and go to "My Orders". Find the item you wish to return and click "Return Item". Fill out the return form and print the prepaid shipping label we provide. Pack your item and drop it off at any shipping location.

Once we receive your returned item and inspect it, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear in your account.

If your product arrives damaged, please contact our customer support immediately with photos of the damage. We'll arrange a replacement or refund without requiring you to ship the damaged item back. Take photos of the damaged packaging and product for our records.
