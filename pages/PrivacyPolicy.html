Privacy Policy

How TechHub collects, uses, and protects your personal information

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechHub. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use our website, create an account, place an order, or contact our support team.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name, email address, password, and profile information provided during registration.
  • Order details: Billing and delivery address, phone number, purchased products, and order history.
  • Payment details: Payment confirmation and transaction status through secure payment channels (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Usage data: Search terms, pages viewed, device/browser information, and interactions that help improve site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your TechHub account.
  • To process orders, coordinate island-wide delivery, and share shipping updates.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To improve product recommendations, website usability, and service quality.
  • To send promotional messages when you have agreed to receive them.

4. Information Sharing

TechHub does not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers when required to operate our business, such as payment gateways, delivery partners, and technical service providers. These partners are expected to handle your data securely and only for approved business purposes.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies to keep your session active, remember preferences, and improve browsing experience. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the website may not work properly.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. While we apply strong security measures, no online system is completely risk-free.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary for account management, order fulfillment, legal obligations, and customer support. When data is no longer required, we securely remove or anonymize it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate account details.
  • Request account deletion, subject to legal and transaction record obligations.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing any personal information.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect service changes, legal requirements, or improvements in our data practices. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact our support team through the Contact page or write to us at privacy@techhub.lk.
